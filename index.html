01 / THE PROBLEM

Wildlife crossing highways creates a problem that detection alone cannot solve.

Traditional surveillance systems can identify animals, but they often fail to translate a detection into an immediate safety action.

PRANSTAMBH introduces a risk-based intelligence layer that considers distance, direction, detection duration and highway proximity.
